## 1. Purpose and Philosophy

Woodwell is committed to an equitable, consistent, and transparent promotion process. We aim to recognize not only scientific excellence but also organizational engagement. Promotions are based on merit, demonstrated growth, and the individual's potential to succeed in an increased scope of responsibility.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements

To be considered for a promotion, employees must generally meet the following criteria:

Tenure: Minimum of one (1) year in current position.

Performance: A record of strong performance with no active performance or disciplinary issues.

Budgetary Alignment: Promotion requests should be accounted for in the relevant fiscal year budget.

All promotions and awards remain at the discretion of the President and CEO.

## 4. Required Promotion Package Materials

All packages must be submitted via Jotform.

### A. Standard Requirements (All Tiers)

Updated CV

Cover Letter addressing the criteria for advancement in each role (see Position Specific Criteria)

Supervisor letter of support advocating for the promotion, recapping past performance, and detailing professional growth.*

Organizational engagement indicators will be considered (see Section 5).

*Note: In instances where a supervisor does not support a promotion, the employee may still submit their package for review. The hiring committee will review on a case-by-case basis.

### B. Advanced Requirements (Research Scientist I and Above)

The required number of external reference letters reflecting the candidate's scope of work and influence in the scientific community. (Refer to the chart in the P&C Promotions section on the intranet for specific requirements and sample reference letters).

One (1) letter of reference from a Woodwell staff member outside of the Research team (a colleague who does not report up to a VP of Science).

Note on References: All letters (external and internal) should be requested directly by the candidate in advance and uploaded to the Jotform at the time of submission. If confidentiality is requested, the reference can submit the letter directly to peopleandculture@woodwellclimate.org.

## 5. Organizational Engagement Metric

Woodwell values operational reliability. The following data points are included in the review process as indicators of an individual's engagement with the Center's ecosystem:

Timeliness of timecard submissions.

Timeliness of credit card expense reports (if applicable).

Completion of required trainings (e.g., KnowBe4).

Completion of performance reviews for subordinates (if applicable).

While these metrics do not disqualify a candidate, they are considered during the committee's deliberation.

## 6. Review and Decision-Making Process

To ensure institutional consistency, the Promotion Committee will consist of the three VPs of Science and the VP of People & Culture (P&C). The promotion committee uses a standard template for decision-making that supports promotions based on merit.

## 7. Post-Committee Review and Notification

Once the Promotion Committee has reached a decision, the following notification chain will be initiated:

Supervisor Notification: People & Culture (P&C) will notify the candidate's supervisor of the decision, providing context and offering support for the supervisor to share the decision with the employee.

Employee Notification: The supervisor is responsible for communicating the outcome to the employee, preferably in a formal meeting.

If promotion is approved, the supervisor will receive a letter from the P&C team including new title and compensation. The supervisor will deliver this letter to the employee celebrating the achievement and discussing any transition of duties.

If promotion is denied, the supervisor will communicate the decision and rationale to the employee and discuss next steps. A denial is not intended to be a permanent barrier. If a promotion is denied, the supervisor should provide guidance and specific milestones required for the employee to be re-evaluated for promotion at a future cycle.

## 8. Equal Opportunity

All promotion decisions are made without regard to status in any group protected by applicable federal, state, or local law. Woodwell reserves the right to fill positions through external searches if it is determined to be in the best interest of the Center.

## 9. Position Specific Criteria

### A. Research Assistant/Research Associate Promotion Criteria

For promotions to Research Assistants I, II, and III and Research Associates I, II, and Senior Research Associates please address in your cover letter:

Excellence in core competencies of the specific position expectations

Growth in skills

If consistent with the employee's position criteria, progressive participation in identifying possible grants and contributing to the proposal process

Ability and willingness to teach colleagues

Service to Woodwell and the community

Independence and initiative

Service/time in current level/position

### B. Research Scientist Promotion Criteria

For promotions to Research Scientists I, II, Senior, and Distinguished please address in your cover letter how you have contributed to furthering the mission of Woodwell through the following:

#### Create Knowledge

peer-reviewed academic publications, policy research publications, magazine-style pieces, grey literature, and technical reports.

#### Apply Knowledge

attendance and/or presentation at scientific, academic, policy or professional workshops and/or conferences, policy engagement, advisory work, and service to Woodwell.

#### Share Knowledge

public lectures, webinars, talks, and engagement outside of usual professional meetings and advisory work, service to the broader and scientific communities, educational and public outreach.

#### Leadership

Motivating and supporting staff; inspiring collaborative culture within project team(s) and Woodwell; collaboration cross-functionally and with other organizations; providing critical thinking and innovation for center wide and network initiatives.

#### Proposals and Fundraising

Leading or supporting proposals and being a PI or a Co-PI with a scientist.

#### Technical Skills

Must possess technical skills as appropriate for the position (coding, modeling, lab/field skills) and ambition to grow these skills.

### C. Principal Scientist Promotion Criteria

For all promotions to Assistant Principal Investigator, Associate Principal Investigator, or Senior Principal Investigator, please see the Woodwell Handbook for criteria and process.
